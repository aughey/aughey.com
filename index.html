Professional work

Scale AI · Staff software engineer

AI-assisted video labeling

I built a replacement for a full-motion video labeling application and integrated on-demand segmentation. A contributor can use the model to outline an object while working through the video. I also work on auto-labeling to take repetitive steps out of that workflow.

The engineering question I keep coming back to is where a model saves the contributor work and where the interface should make it easy to review or correct its output.

Boeing · Technical Fellow

Camera pose and geospatial imagery

I led technical work on geospatial research with the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency. One problem was estimating where a camera was and where it was pointing when it captured an image, so aerial imagery could be aligned with a 3D scene.

In one patented approach, I used features visible in the image and in a 3D model to refine the camera pose when the initial estimate was off.
