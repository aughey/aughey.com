About me

How I work

I want to see how a tool gets used before deciding what it should do. In a simulator that might mean understanding what has to happen in real time. In a video labeling tool it means paying attention to what a person has to do over and over.

At Scale AI, I work on tools for labeling full-motion video. Before that, I spent 28 years at Boeing, working on real-time systems, simulation, and geospatial tools. As a Technical Fellow, I helped teams adopt Rust and taught workshops on memory-safe programming.

I like teaching for the same reason I like prototyping. Trying to make an idea concrete exposes the parts I still need to understand. I get to practice that outside software, too, teaching Aikido to kids and adults.
